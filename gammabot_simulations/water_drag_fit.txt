Water Drag Constant Estimation
===============================

Model: a = -c_1*v^2, where c_1 = 0.5*rho*Cd*A/m
Solution: x(t) = ln(1 + c_1*v_0*t) / c_1

Fitted Parameters:
  v_0 = 0.26179 m/s (from data)
  c_1 = 2.5526 1/m
  R^2 = 0.99985

Where:
  c_1 = 0.5*rho*Cd*A/m (drag parameter)
  v_0 = initial velocity
  x(t) = displacement (m)
  t = time (s)
